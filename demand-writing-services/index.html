What goes into a demand package

A demand package is the case your firm presents to the insurance adjuster before negotiation. Your YVA demand writer prepares each part:

  • Medical record review. Reading treatment records, organizing them by provider and date, and summarizing diagnoses, treatment and the course of care.
  • Damages calculation. Compiling medical bills and other specials, lost wages and the other damages your attorney wants included.
  • Demand letter drafting. Writing the letter that lays out liability, injuries and damages, in your firm's voice and to your standards.
  • Settlement package prep. Assembling the letter with its exhibits, records and bills into a complete, organized package.
  • Adjuster follow-up. Tracking response dates and following up with the adjuster so packages do not sit unanswered.

For a longer explanation of the work itself, read what is demand writing.

How the workflow runs

  1. You send the file. Once treatment is complete and records and bills are in, the case goes to the demand writer through your case management system.
  2. Records review. The writer reviews and summarizes the medical records and flags anything missing.
  3. Draft. The writer calculates damages and drafts the demand letter from your template.
  4. Attorney review. An attorney reviews, edits and approves the demand, including the amount demanded.
  5. Send and follow up. The approved package goes out and the writer tracks the adjuster's response.

The attorney stays in charge

Demand writers prepare drafts and packages; they do not give legal advice or decide what a case is worth. Your attorney reviews and approves every demand before it is sent and remains responsible for the work. No staffing service can promise a settlement amount, and YVA does not.

Why a dedicated writer instead of a generalist

In many firms, demands get written by whoever has time, usually a paralegal or an attorney fitting it between other work. Demand writing needs sustained focus: long medical records, careful arithmetic and clear, persuasive writing. When it is squeezed in between calls and filing, packages go out late or get stuck in a backlog.

A dedicated writer works through the queue every day. Your attorneys move from writing demands to reviewing them, which is a far shorter task. Bilingual writers also help when records, statements or client communications are in Spanish.

Matching your firm's standards

Your demand writer does not bring a generic template. During setup, YVA briefs the writer on:

  • Your demand letter templates and preferred structure
  • How you present liability, injuries and damages
  • Which exhibits you include and in what order
  • Who reviews drafts and how edits come back

The first few demands are the calibration period. Your attorney's edits become the standard for the next ones.

Frequently asked questions

What are outsourced demand writing services?

Outsourced demand writing means a remote specialist prepares demand packages for a personal injury firm: reviewing medical records, calculating damages, drafting the demand letter and assembling the settlement package. The firm's attorney reviews and approves each demand before it is sent.

How much does outsourced demand writing cost?

YVA Law Staffing bills demand writing at $10 an hour. A full-time demand writer working 160 hours a month costs about $1,600 a month.

Will the demand letters match our firm's style?

Yes. Writers draft to your firm's templates and standards, and your attorney's edits on early drafts set the pattern for later ones.

Does the demand writer decide the settlement amount?

No. The writer prepares the damages calculation and draft, but the attorney decides the demand amount and approves the package. Demand writers do not give legal advice.

Every YVA demand writer is fully bilingual in English and Spanish, so they can work with Spanish-language statements, records and client communications.
