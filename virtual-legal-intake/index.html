What a virtual intake specialist does

Intake is the first conversation a potential client has with your firm. The specialist's job is to make that conversation fast, complete and consistent, then hand your attorneys a clean file.

  • Answers calls and emails in English or Spanish, in whichever language the caller prefers.
  • Screens and qualifies each inquiry using the questions and eligibility rules your firm sets: type of matter, dates, location, opposing party, conflicts information you want collected.
  • Completes your intake form and enters the contact into your CRM or case management system, so nothing lives on a sticky note.
  • Schedules consultations directly on your attorneys' calendars.
  • Follows up on leads who did not book, did not return documents, or went quiet after the first call.

Why intake deserves a dedicated person

Most firms do not lose cases because the attorney said no. They lose them because the phone rang while everyone was busy, the voicemail was returned the next afternoon, or a Spanish-speaking caller could not find anyone to talk to. A potential client who reaches no one often calls the next firm on the list.

A dedicated intake specialist removes those gaps: calls are answered by someone whose only job is intake, every inquiry follows the same script, and follow-ups happen on schedule. If your firm serves Spanish-speaking communities, bilingual intake on every shift matters even more. Our article on bilingual staff for law firms covers why.

A typical day (example)

A common pattern, depending on your volume and practice area:

  1. Start of shift: review overnight web forms, voicemails and emails; call back every new lead.
  2. Through the day: answer live calls, screen callers, complete intake forms and CRM entries, book consultations.
  3. Between calls: chase missing documents and signed forms, send reminders for upcoming consultations.
  4. End of shift: send the attorney a short summary of new leads, booked consults and anyone who needs a decision.

Intake specialists do not give legal advice

Your specialist collects facts, follows your screening script and books consultations. They do not evaluate claims, quote case values or give legal advice. They work under your attorneys' supervision, and the attorney decides which matters the firm accepts.

Frequently asked questions

What does a virtual legal intake specialist do?

A virtual legal intake specialist answers a law firm's calls and emails, screens potential clients against the firm's criteria, completes intake forms and CRM entries, schedules consultations and follows up on leads. They work remotely under attorney supervision.

Can a virtual intake specialist cover nights and weekends?

Yes. YVA intake specialists work U.S. time zones, and coverage is available 24/7 across U.S. time zones, so after-hours calls can be answered by a person instead of voicemail.

How much does a virtual legal intake specialist cost?

YVA Law Staffing charges $8.50 an hour for legal intake. A full-time specialist working 160 hours a month costs about $1,360 a month.

Is it allowed to outsource legal intake?

Generally, yes, as long as the attorney supervises the work and protects client confidentiality. ABA Model Rule 5.3 covers supervising nonlawyer assistants and Rule 1.6 covers confidentiality. Check your state bar's guidance for the rules that apply to your firm.

What happens if the specialist is not a good fit?
