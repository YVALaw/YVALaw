Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who We Are

YVA Law Staffing ("we", "us", "our") operates at yvastaffing.agency. We provide bilingual virtual staffing solutions for U.S. law firms and professional services. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at contact@yvastaffing.agency.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact and booking forms, including:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number, if you provide it for calls or SMS messages
  • Company name and website
  • Service inquiries and messages
  • Scheduling information via Calendly

We also collect anonymized analytics data, such as page views and traffic sources, via Google Analytics to improve our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Send service-related calls or SMS messages when you opt in
  • Send relevant information about our services
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. SMS Messaging

If you provide your mobile phone number and opt in, you consent to receive recurring SMS messages from YVA Law Staffing about inquiries, appointments, service updates, applications, and related business communications.

  • Message frequency varies based on your request, relationship with us, and active communications.
  • You may reply STOP to opt out of future SMS messages.
  • You may reply HELP for more information.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

We do not share SMS opt-in data, consent, or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies

We use:

  • Essential cookies - required for the site to function
  • Analytics cookies - Google Analytics, anonymized, to understand traffic patterns

You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to use the site.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services that may process your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Calendly - appointment scheduling
  • EmailJS - secure form submission delivery
  • Google Analytics - anonymized website analytics

7. Data Retention

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us at contact@yvastaffing.agency.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@yvastaffing.agency and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
