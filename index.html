Our Story

Built by People Who Lived the Problem.

YVA Law Staffing didn't start in a boardroom — it started on the floor of a California law firm. Hans Esmel, our CEO, was working closely with legal teams when the demand for quality bilingual talent became impossible to ignore. What started as informal referrals quickly revealed something bigger: a rare ability to identify, vet, and train high-performing professionals who integrated seamlessly into demanding legal environments.

As Hans studied the market, one thing stood out — competitors were charging a premium for the same quality of service that could be delivered at a fraction of the cost. Small and mid-size law firms were being priced out of the support they needed to grow.

In 2023, Hans founded YVA Law Staffing with a clear mission: help law firms scale their teams without burning their budget. Operating from a physical office in the Dominican Republic, YVA now provides fully bilingual, rigorously vetted virtual professionals who work in U.S. time zones and integrate directly into your firm's workflow — not as freelancers, but as real team members.
